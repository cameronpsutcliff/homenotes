# HomeNotes Glossary

- **Address Key (`address_key`):** Lowercase, punctuation-stripped, street-suffix-canonicalized representation of a street address (e.g., `123 n main st hayden id 83835`) used to unify threads across Zillow, Redfin, and Realtor.com.
- **Fair Value Estimate (`fair_value`):** Optional dollar figure submitted by a buyer representing what they believe the property is worth, aggregated into a community median and compared against the asking price.
- **Price Snapshot (`listed_price`):** The asking price captured from the listing page at the exact moment a comment is posted, preserving price-cut history.
- **Signal Tags (`tags`):** Allowlisted quick-tag labels (`Overpriced`, `Flip Special`, `Price Cut Needed`, `Needs Work`, `Fair Value`, `Great Lot / Shop`, `Dealbreaker`, `Top Pick`) attached to buyer comments.
