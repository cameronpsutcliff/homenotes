HomeNotes

Platform-independent buyer reality-check comments, price-cut tracking, live FRED 30-year mortgage math, and US Census county verification across 7 major US real estate portals.

Extension Surface

  • extension/manifest.json: MV3 manifest targeting 7 portals (Zillow, Redfin, Realtor, Homes.com, Trulia, HAR, BofA).
  • extension/lib/extractor.js: JSON-LD + DOM extraction, cross-site address key normalization, CPI + 4% climb math, P&I calculator.
  • extension/content.js: Isolated Abyssal Shadow-DOM draggable pill and full-height docked side panel.
  • extension/popup.html: Settings and recent community reality checks.

Edge API ($0/mo)

  • worker/src/index.js: Deployed at https://homenotes.cameronsutcliff.com.
  • GET /api/comments: Lookup by normalized address or author handle + Price-Cut summary.
  • GET /api/geocode: US Census Geocoder (canonical USPS address + County Assessor link).
  • GET /api/market: Live FRED MORTGAGE30US 30Y rate (24h edge cache).
  • POST /api/comments/:id/agree & /flag: Upvotes and self-moderation.
